Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

The Sports Hub is a team app for youth baseball and softball: scoring, stats, schedules, team chat and coaching tools. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, the name you enter, and the teams you belong to with your role on each (owner, coach, family, scorer or camera).
  • Team information that coaches and families add: roster names and numbers, game scores and stats, schedules, practices, attendance, announcements, chat messages, chat photos, tournament fee records and, if a coach adds them, player birthdays (used only for the team's birthday greeting).
  • Notifications: if you turn on notifications, a device token so we can send scores, chat and announcements to your phone, plus your notification settings.
  • Paid plans: when a team owner buys a plan in the iPhone app, Apple handles the payment. We receive the subscription's status, product and renewal date from Apple, never your card or Apple ID details.
  • Swing AI: swing videos are analyzed on your phone and stay there. Only the measurements (such as timing numbers) are saved to the team.

How we use it

Only to run the app for your team: showing games and stats to the people your coach approves, delivering chat and notifications, turning on the features of the team's plan, and keeping the app secure. We don't sell personal information and we don't show ads.

Who can see it

Team information is visible only to people the team's coaches approve, and to anyone the coach lets watch games (with a watch link or the "Anyone with the link" setting). Each team's data is kept separate from every other team's.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase stores accounts and team data and delivers notifications.
  • Cloudflare runs our server, stores chat photos and streams live video.
  • Anthropic reads roster photos and writes scouting reports when an Elite team's coach asks for them. These requests contain only what the coach sends.
  • Apple processes in-app purchases.
  • YouTube (Google), only if a coach connects the team's own YouTube channel for game video uploads.

Children

Accounts are for adults: coaches, parents and guardians. Players' names, numbers and stats are added by their coaches and families. If you believe a child created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Keeping and deleting your information

You can delete your account any time in the app: tap Teams, then Delete account. That removes your sign-in, your place on every team and your notification settings. Team records (games, stats, schedules) belong to the team and stay for its other members. A team owner can ask us to delete a whole team. Paid plans are canceled in your Apple ID's Subscriptions settings.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support@example.com

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
